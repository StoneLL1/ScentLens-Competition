PROJECT CONSTRAINTS:
The input is an already planned scene. Do not reinterpret scent scores or redesign it.

Preserve every selected entity, its motif, stated count or grouping, emphasis,
placement, composition, and spatial relations. Do not add or substitute objects,
scent sources, materials, weather, locations, actions, or decorative scenery.
A neutral unspecified background or support must remain plain and indeterminate.

Use one dominant subject when the input has one primary entity. If the input mode
is dual, preserve both co-primary entities with comparable importance instead of
forcing a single winner. If the primary entity is an environment, let that
scent-bearing environment remain dominant. If it is the only entity, do not
invent a foreground object. An environmental accent must remain localized.

Treat 3–5 major shapes and 2–4 colors as simplification targets, not quotas.
Fewer shapes or colors are acceptable for a sparse scene. Do not add objects to
fill the budget. A compact group may read as one mass, but preserve its named
members and stated count. Do not delete selected accent entities as tiny details.

Retain the object names and minimum recognizable silhouettes. Simplify grass into
a continuous grassy green field without blades, a selected lavender patch into
joined violet masses, and selected cedarwood into broad brown wooden forms without
fine grain. The sky, ocean, and forest examples apply only when explicitly present
in the input; they do not authorize adding those settings. Clove means dried spice
buds, not lilac flowers. Vanilla means the selected pods, not cake or ice cream.
Exception: when a selected motif phrase itself names a memory-anchor object
(mulled wine, orange pomander, sharpened pencil, vanilla ice cream, vanilla
cream), that anchor object is explicitly authorized for that scent only; the
anchor must stay clearly tied to its named scent source, and all other
blacklisted contexts remain forbidden.

When ascii.enabled is true, mention luminous ASCII only inside its specified target
forms. Keep other regions almost free of ASCII. If a target is an environment,
confine ASCII to a bounded focal region within that existing environment.
When ascii.enabled is false, do not mention or request ASCII.

Only the coarse scene content and ASCII placement belong here. Do not invent a
new visual style or repeat the full photographic, grain, blur, or bloom treatment;
the fixed style is appended later. Use natural object colors unless broad color
hints are explicitly supplied; do not calculate colors or light from confidence.

Output one concise English paragraph, preferably 40–120 words. Do not output JSON,
Markdown, headings, explanations, scores, internal IDs, or readable image labels.
