The main subject is reconstructed using dense monospaced ASCII characters, numbers, punctuation, and simple symbols, which function like glowing pixels rather than readable text. The glyphs closely follow the subject’s contours, edges, highlights, shadows, and internal structures. Character density varies with brightness: bright areas contain dense glowing white or softly tinted glyphs, midtones contain moderate character density, while dark areas become sparse and partially disappear. The ASCII characters should feel embedded in the image, not overlaid on top of it. Retain subtle grid alignment and digital raster structure while allowing outer contours to remain irregular and organic. The characters should have slightly softened edges, strong bloom, glowing halos, slight overexposure, and soft optical blur. Keep the background visually quiet, blurred, and essentially free of ASCII characters. Create a strong exposure contrast between the glowing typographic subject and the soft photographic background.
